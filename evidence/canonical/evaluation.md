# Synthetic adversarial evaluation

- Scenarios: 127 (120 multi-round baselines + 1 ablation control + 6 same-seed feature ablations)
- Cohort sizes: 10, 100, and 1,000 virtual participants
- Minimum normalized entropy: 0.792302
- Maximum adversarial AUC (unfavorable): 1.000000

Synthetic results do not prove real-world anonymity. Larger announced cohorts are not automatically larger observed anonymity sets; dropouts and unique features reduce the effective set. Synchronization can itself be a detectable fingerprint.
